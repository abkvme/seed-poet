How strong is a poem seed?

A Seed Poet poem is a valid BIP39 phrase, but it is a little weaker than a fully random one. Rhymes, syllable counts and the verb, adjective, noun pattern limit which words can go where.

  • 12 words: about 88 bits of randomness (fully random BIP39: 128 bits)
  • 24 words: about 176 bits (fully random BIP39: 256 bits)

Even 88 bits is about 3 × 1026 possible poems, far beyond any known brute-force attack, including one by someone who knows you used Seed Poet. These figures are our estimate.

Our advice: for a wallet that holds real value, use 24 words, and for the highest safety use the offline version on a computer without internet.
