BIP39 word list, by rhyme and by number

BIP39 (Bitcoin Improvement Proposal 39) is the standard that turns a wallet's secret into a recovery phrase of 12 or 24 words. Every word comes from one fixed list of {{.Wordlist.Total}} English words. The first four letters of each word are unique, so a wallet can recognise a word from its start, and the last word carries a checksum.

This page shows the whole English list, sorted by sound instead of alphabet. The first part holds the rhyme groups that Seed Poet uses to end the lines of its poems: the nouns at the end of two lines of a couplet always come from the same group. The second part puts every other word next to the words that end the same way. At the end comes the whole list in official order, numbered, for looking up a word's number.

Never pick seed words yourself: people choose badly and a chosen phrase is easy to guess. Let a generator use real randomness. Write a 24-word poem and every word on it will be on this list.
